Taxes & GST

Final payable nearest whole rupee par round hota hai (jaise ₹239.45 → ₹239, ₹254.65 → ₹255). Har receipt par "Round Off ±x.xx" line aati hai.

GST / Tax Categories

Apne menu items ke GST slabs tay karein (jaise "No Tax" 0%, GST 5%, 12%, 18%). Item ke edit page par slab assign karein; jis item par slab nahi hai wo default slab use karta hai — abhi aapka restaurant rate — (upar ke CGST + SGST se).

Waiter permissions

Allow waiters to replace items

ON hone par waiter kitchen me abhi ban rahe item ko badal sakta hai (purana item void, naya fire, aur cash ka difference reconcile) — wahi flow jo owners/managers use karte hain. OFF par sirf owners, managers aur supervisors items replace kar sakte hain. Default OFF.

Payment Methods

UPI ID / VPA (static QR)

Static QR ki tarah dikhta hai — apne aap payment confirm nahi hoti; haath se reconcile karein.

Auto-confirm UPI (online payment provider)

Payment provider jod kar kiosk par har order ka dynamic QR banayein aur gateway payment capture karte hi order auto-confirm ho.

Provider

🔒 Key Secret aur Webhook Secret write-only hain — ye page par kabhi wapas nahi dikhte. Khali chhodne par purana secret rehta hai.

Isse apne provider dashboard me webhook / payment-status callback URL ki jagah paste karein.

⚠️ Gateway integration abhi active nahi hai: keys sirf save hoti hain. Webhook se payment auto-confirm aur "Test connection" abhi kaam nahi karte. Keys database me plain text me store hoti hain.

Wait for payment confirmation

ON hone par ye surface UPI payment callback ka intezaar karta hai (paisa sach me aaya) tabhi order complete/settle hota hai. OFF par payment turant record ho jaati hai aur staff receipt haath se verify karta hai — self-serve screens par "I've Paid" button dikhta hai. Rush me kisi surface ko OFF kar sakte hain.

Use online gateway (Razorpay) per surface

Kaun si ordering surfaces static UPI QR ki jagah aapke connected online provider se charge karein. Ye sirf wahan lagta hai jahan provider connected aur enabled ho. Customer app aur QR table dine-in ke liye recommended.
